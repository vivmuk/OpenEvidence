ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and OpenEvidence vs 2024 AAOS Meniscus Guidelines — The Knee (Aug 2026, PMID 41865615)
Four-way head-to-head against 9 AAOS CPG recommendations for acute isolated meniscal pathology. Three blinded sports-medicine specialists (Cochran's Q, Fleiss' kappa, majority-vote resolution). OpenEvidence achieved the highest AAOS concordance of the four systems tested.

Bias & Safety
Sociodemographic Bias in LLM Dizziness Decisions — J Vestib Res (Aug 8, PMID 42569795)
100 ED dizziness vignettes × 34 sociodemographic variants × 12 instruction-tuned LLMs × 10 reps = 2,040,000 responses (TiTrATE / GRACE-3 / Bárány frameworks). Quantifies descriptor-driven deviations in LLM triage / neuroimaging — adversarial test methodology applicable to OE-style consumer-facing tools.
Benchmark & CoT Failure Audit
Chain-of-Thought Failure Patterns Across Model Families — J Med Syst (Aug 8, PMID 42570145)
Mixed-methods audit of o4-mini + Gemini 2.5 Flash on 3,060 COVID-19 vaccination tweets; FUTURE-AI framework; 14.9% dual-error cases produced identical wrong reasoning across both models. Demonstrates shared logic gaps not fixable by ensembling — directly relevant for clinical LLMs that expose chain-of-thought.
Narrative Synthesis
Clinician-Facing AI & Guideline Adherence Narrative Review — J Diabetes Metab Disord (Aug 8, PMID 42571079)
SANRA-aligned structured narrative synthesis (2020–May 2026): only 6 peer-reviewed empirical studies met eligibility. Strongest evidence in bounded, workflow-embedded settings (EHR-integrated CDSS, decision-tree). Frames OpenEvidence-style RAG guidance within the broader CDSS evidence stack.
