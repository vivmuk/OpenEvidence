Benchmarking / Meta-Analysis
EClinicalMedicine publishes largest AI cardiovascular RCT meta-analysis — 31 trials, 1.69M patients (Aug 6, PMID 42602970)
Ong et al. (Imperial / Oxford / King's College London) publish the most comprehensive systematic review and meta-analysis of AI in cardiovascular care to date: 31 RCTs across 13 regions (n=1,685,717 patients). Image-based AI-CDSS reduces MACE (RR 0.74, 95% CI 0.58-0.96; I²=0); AI-mobile health interventions reduce systolic BP by 3.18 mmHg; AI-enhanced ECG does not significantly improve heart failure detection (RR 1.22, I²=90%). 71% of trials reported significant AI benefit, but almost all on intermediate process endpoints rather than hard outcomes; GRADE certainty remains low-to-very-low across outcomes. Directly informs the clinical-evidence argument for OpenEvidence-style evidence-lookup tools: real-world AI-CDSS can move hard outcomes when wedded to imaging workflows — but does not yet generalize across modalities. Pivotal Lancet-family evidence for regulators, payers, and hospital procurement.
OE Product Launch — Gamification
OpenEvidence Games Launched — MedMini and Synapses (Aug 10, 2026)
OpenEvidence launches OpenEvidence Games — a new suite of puzzles built for clinicians, live now in Discover on web and in the OpenEvidence app. The lineup starts with two games: MedMini and Synapses, bringing gamified medical knowledge reinforcement to hundreds of thousands of verified clinicians. Extends the platform beyond clinical decision support into medical education and engagement, competing with traditional CME platforms and medical learning apps.

OpenEvidence Doctor Dialer wide-release — AI-integrated telemedicine
Wide-release of AI-integrated Doctor Dialer for privacy-centric, doctor-patient telemedicine with clinical decision AI deeply integrated. Adds messaging, faxing, and voicemail. Telemedicine wedge competes with Doximity Dialer, Amwell, K Health (PatientGPT), and EHR vendor virtual-care modules. Together with Visits, extends OE toward full-stack synchronous care.
Benchmarking / Safety Audit
Nature Medicine publishes SIM-VAIL chatbot safety audit — 9 frontier LLMs, 810 conversations (Aug 7, PMID 42567928)
Weilnhammer et al. publish SIM-VAIL (simulated vulnerability-amplifying interaction loops) framework. 810 conversations across 9 frontier consumer chatbots (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Llama families) and 30 simulated psychiatric profiles scored across 13 clinically-grounded risk dimensions. Concerning behavior widespread but reduced in newer models. First clinically-validated cross-vendor consumer chatbot safety audit — explicitly applicable to evaluating ChatGPT Health and Copilot Health, and to risk-stratification training for clinician-facing CDS. Microsoft AI and UK AI Security Institute staff among authors.
Implementation Framework
PACT framework for clinical AI continuity-of-care (Npj Health Syst, Aug 5, PMID 42552388)
Khairat and Safran (UNC + Harvard Medical School) publish PACT: Patient-centered, AI-enabled Continuity and Timely action. Reframes clinical AI as a health-system function spanning pre-visit, visit, and post-visit care with explicit ownership, communication, confirmation, escalation, and outcome measures. Post-visit coordination identified as the highest-impact near-term setting with tiered human support. Academic consensus signal that OE-style CDS will need to extend into longitudinal care continuity.
Singapore Hospital AI Discontinued — Cautionary
JMIR: Singapore tertiary hospital chatbot discontinued due to sociotechnical misalignment (Aug 1, PMID 42573583)
Yeow et al. publish single-hospital case study (16 semi-structured interviews, Davis et al. sociotechnical systems framework) of ChatAI deployment at a large Singapore government tertiary hospital. Misalignments across goals, people, processes, technology, infrastructure led to eventual discontinuation despite initial implementation efforts. Cautionary cautionary evidence that successful launch ≠ sustained adoption — applies to all major regional hospital AI rollouts (NHS, ABDM, AIIMS Smart Doctor, NHS 505K Copilot).
