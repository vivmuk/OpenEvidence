Head-to-Head Study — Otology / Tinnitus (OE WINS)
OpenEvidence Ranks #1 in Tinnitus AI Quality Study — Otol Neurotol (Jul 20, PMID 42473942)
30 tinnitus questions from Google Trends (2020-2025) evaluated across 6 GenAI systems (OpenEvidence, Claude, DeepSeek, GPT-5, Gemini, GPT-4) + Google Search. 6 expert raters using QAMAI framework. OpenEvidence achieved the highest mean quality score (4.45±0.72), significantly better than all other systems (Friedman P<0.001; pairwise effect size r=0.49-0.86). Claude 2nd (4.00), DeepSeek 3rd (3.92), Google Search last (2.27). Notable inverse readability finding: OE at postgraduate reading level (Flesch-Kincaid Grade 17.5) vs general LLMs at 6th-7th grade — clinician-grade content is less patient-accessible.
OE in Research Methodology
Duke Maternal Morbidity Review Uses OpenEvidence for Evidence Acquisition — Obstet Gynecol Surv (Aug 1, PMID 42561102)
Duke University narrative review of long-term sequelae of severe maternal morbidity explicitly used OpenEvidence searches alongside PubMed as structured evidence acquisition methodology. Documents cardiovascular, renal, psychiatric, and reproductive risks post-SMM. Signifies growing adoption of OE as a research tool in academic literature.
