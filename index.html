Benchmark Study
Full Stanford–Harvard ARISE NOHARM Revealed-Preference Result — Jul 20
NOHARM study (45 LLMs + 4 clinical AI systems, 12,747 expert annotations, 4,249 potential clinical actions). In randomized free-choice arm of 101 board-certified US physicians, OpenEvidence was reached for in 22.3% of responses — more than ChatGPT + Claude + Gemini + every other external AI combined (19.8%). Revealed physician preference complements the (negative) Nature Medicine benchmark results. OE framing: adoption is earned one accurate answer at a time; OE has never paid or incentivized any physician. Caveat: not peer-reviewed. Doximity Ask arm: number of physician-users unreleased.
Product Launch
OpenEvidence EvidenceGrade™ — GRADE-Based Citation-Quality Grading (Jul 10)
Real-time evidence-quality grading and visualization beneath every OE answer. Builds on GRADE framework (Cochrane, WHO, major guideline bodies). Solves AI problem of glossing over RCT vs observational differences at the point of care. Direct differentiator vs ChatGPT for Clinicians, UpToDate Expert AI, Dyna AI, ClinicalKey AI which do not surface per-citation evidence strength. Companion to Cochrane and AAO-HNSF clinical-guideline partnerships.
