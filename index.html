Benchmark Study
OE Last in 5-LLM Pediatric Hypospadias Test (But 100% Citation Accuracy) — JMIR PMID 42525870
Cross-sectional comparison of ChatGPT-4o Gemini-2.5-Pro OpenEvidence Zhipu Qingyan and DeepSeek on 10 pediatric hypospadias perioperative questions. Evaluated by 23 pediatric urology experts and 36 caregivers. Gemini-2.5-Pro ranked 1st (median 5.0) DeepSeek 2nd. OpenEvidence scored lowest overall (2.0) despite 100% verifiable citation accuracy. But in an 8-reviewer clinical safety audit OE carried 5 of 9 Severe-level safety flags — the largest severe burden of any model tested. Citation integrity and clinical safety are dissociable.
Adoption Study
OpenEvidence #2 Among 2nd-Year US Med Students — S D Med PMID 42526014
USD Sanford SOM survey (n=28): ChatGPT most-used at ~90% followed by OpenEvidence. Uses: diagnostic test suggestions management strategies knowledge clarification. Concerns: overreliance lost independent reasoning inaccurate outputs. OE has now crossed into pre-clinical medical-student workflow complementing its 50%+ physician adoption figure.
Landscape Study
8,532 Clinical AI Trials Mapped — arXiv:2607.22607
LLM-classifier analysis of ClinicalTrials.gov. 80% of trials post-2019; 30.5% RCT. Imaging AI is the largest single modality (29%) but clinical text/NLP trials are up 7x since 2018. Prognostic AI (4,324) > Diagnostic (3,828). Treatment-recommendation tier only 9% (768 trials) — the domain most relevant to OE-class CDS tools remains evidence-thin. Translational maturity limited: 38% retrospective 21% silent prospective; only 184 Level-4 semi-autonomous trials (68% glucose-mgmt).
Regulatory
EU AI Act Annex IV Transparency Obligations In Force (2 Aug 2026)
EU AI Act Annex IV transparency obligations begin applying 2 Aug 2026 — direct effect on EU-facing clinical AI chat tools. OpenEvidence remains geoblocked in EU and UK. ChatGPT Health for Clinicians UpToDate Expert AI and other frontier clinical-chat products must comply to operate in EU market. OE's compliance pathway via the NEJM-JAMA-NCCN ties not yet visible.
